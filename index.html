Privacy & safety

Nothing about your walk is uploaded for inference. Preferences stay on this device. Network needs: one-time model download and Transformers.js. No analytics, no accounts, no geolocation. History lives in localStorage.

Every model suggestion passes a plain-code checker. Unsafe lines are swapped from a curated offline bank. The filter is a heuristic — use judgment outdoors.
